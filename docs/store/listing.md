## Short description (under 100 characters)
Read any text aloud in natural AI voices that run on your PC, with every word highlighted.

## Description

No subscription, no account, no cloud: pxd Speak's voices run offline on your PC, and nothing you read is sent anywhere.

Paste an article, an e-mail or a chapter and press play. Each word lights up as it's heard, the sentence is tinted, and the page scrolls along with you. Click any word to jump there. It's easier to follow, easier to focus, and kind to tired eyes.

Read anywhere: select text in any app and press Ctrl + Alt + R. Add the free pxd Speak extension for Edge or Chrome and words light up right on the web page as they're read.

Voices that sound human: 28 Kokoro voices (US and UK, female and male) in one download, 20 light Piper voices, plus the voices already in Windows. Change the speed from 0.5× to 2×.

Made for people who listen while they work, readers with dyslexia or low vision, language learners, and anyone who would rather hear a long page than read it.

pxd Speak Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) adds: save as MP3 or WAV, read whole files (TXT, Markdown, Word, PDF, EPUB) by dragging them in, and turn a book or document into audiobook files, one per chapter.

## Notes for certification (Partner Center › Submission options › Notes for certification)
pxd Speak runs as a full-trust desktop app (runFullTrust). It registers global hotkeys (Ctrl+Alt+R reads the selected
text in any app) and copies the selection briefly to read it, restoring the clipboard afterwards. Voice models are
downloaded on request from https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases (data only, no code). It listens on
127.0.0.1:47321 for the optional pxd Speak browser extension; it accepts only browser-extension origins and never
accepts connections from other machines. To test Pro: buy the "pxd Speak Pro" add-on in the test account, then the
Read tab's Save / Open file / Audiobook buttons unlock.
