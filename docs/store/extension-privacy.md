# pxd Speak browser extension: privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

The pxd Speak extension for Edge and Chrome highlights each word on a web page while the pxd Speak app on your PC reads
your selection aloud.

- **What it reads:** only the text you select, and only when you ask it to read (the hotkey, or right-click › Read
  aloud with pxd Speak).
- **Where it goes:** to the pxd Speak app on the same computer, over `ws://127.0.0.1:47321`. `127.0.0.1` is the PC
  itself; nothing is sent over the internet, to pxd, or to anyone else.
- **What it keeps:** nothing. It stores no data, history or settings, and it uses no cookies, analytics or ads.
- **Pages:** it adds word highlighting to the page while reading and removes it afterwards, leaving the page as it was.
  It doesn't read forms, passwords or anything you didn't select.
- **Selling or sharing data:** never. No data is collected, so none is sold, shared or used for anything else.
